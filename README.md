# B2B SaaS LinkedIn Research

This repository contains my personal research into B2B SaaS marketing strategies on LinkedIn. The goal is to identify patterns, content structures, and proven growth tactics used by industry leaders.

## Research Objectives
This project focuses on building a repeatable **LinkedIn organic content strategy** specifically for the **B2B SaaS** industry. The objectives are:

1. **Content Strategy Mapping:** Reverse-engineering how top SaaS leaders structure their LinkedIn feed to move beyond "vanity metrics" and build genuine industry authority.
2. **B2B SaaS Messaging Frameworks:** Identifying the specific language, tone, and storytelling techniques that resonate with SaaS buyers (ICP) and decision-makers on LinkedIn.
3. **Organic Distribution Mechanics:** Analyzing how to leverage LinkedIn’s native features (carousels, long-form text, native video) to maximize reach without paid acquisition.
4. **Conversion-Centric Copywriting:** Developing a playbook for high-converting organic posts that effectively bridge the gap between "providing value" and "driving inbound discovery/DMs."
5. **Brand Authority vs. Product Features:** Investigating the balance between teaching industry expertise and showcasing SaaS product utility to drive organic interest.
