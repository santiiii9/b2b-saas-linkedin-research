## Key Tactics (Frameworks & 'How-To')

### 1. Why the Traditional ToFu-MoFu-BoFu Model Fails B2B SaaS

The conventional funnel-based content model structures posts as:

| Stage          | Content Type                          | Goal                    |
|----------------|---------------------------------------|-------------------------|
| Top of Funnel  | Broad personal stories, inspiration   | Attract large audience  |
| Middle Funnel  | Industry/expertise content            | Build authority         |
| Bottom Funnel  | Offer/conversion content              | Sell                    |

**Four reasons this model underperforms for niche B2B SaaS:**

1. **Wrong audience accumulation** — broad ToFu content attracts people 
   with no interest in your specific product or category
2. **Feed poisoning** — a non-ICP audience won't engage with expertise 
   or offer content, causing reach to collapse when it matters most
3. **Engagement addiction** — creators become dependent on viral personal 
   stories because that's the only content their poorly-matched audience 
   responds to
4. **Algorithm shift** — LinkedIn is increasingly prioritising expert and 
   industry content over personal lifestyle stories in 2026

---

### 2. The Authority First Framework

A complete reordering of content priorities: lead with expertise, 
layer in personality second, and convert third.

#### Content Mix Breakdown:

| Content Type         | Share | Purpose                                              |
|----------------------|-------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Industry/Expert      | 70%   | Build authority and attract a qualified ICP audience |
| Personal Stories     | 15%   | Humanise the brand; connect at a personal level      |
| Offer/Conversion     | 15%   | Convert authority and trust into pipeline            |

---

### 3. Pillar One — Industry & Expert Content (70%)

The foundation of the entire framework. Every insight published should 
come from direct experience or expertise — not repurposed from generic 
sources, AI summaries, or competitor content.

**Formats that work within this pillar:**
- Opinions and contrarian takes on industry trends
- Original analysis of market developments
- How-to posts addressing a specific, niche problem
- Infographics illustrating a process or framework unique to your work
- Trend breakdowns with your own commentary

> **Core rule:** If the insight could have been written by anyone or 
> found anywhere else, it does not belong in this pillar. Uniqueness 
> is the competitive moat.

**Mindset shift required:**
Stop optimising for follower growth. Optimise for authority signal. 
The right 10,000 followers who are genuinely in your ICP are worth 
more commercially than 100,000 followers who are not.

---

### 4. Pillar Two — Personal Stories (15%)

Introduced *after* authority has been established — not as the entry 
point. Personalisation amplifies a credible brand; it cannot substitute 
for one.

**Content formats for this pillar:**
- How you got to where you are — career trajectory, inflection points
- Behind the scenes of how you work (process, tools, routines)
- Building in public — company milestones, failures, pivots
- Opinions on your own journey that connect to your industry expertise

> **Why this works at 15%:** Personal stories place you in a monopoly 
> position — no competitor can replicate your specific experiences, 
> opinions, and voice. Combined with established authority, this is 
> what drives inbound conversion uplift.

---

### 5. Pillar Three — Offer & Conversion Content (15%)

The mechanism for turning authority and impressions into qualified 
pipeline. Uses the **PAS Framework** (Problem → Agitate → Solution):

#### The PAS Framework Applied to LinkedIn Posts:

**Step 1 — Problem**
Open with a real, specific problem your ICP faces. Use a client quote 
or a concrete example to make it tangible.

**Step 2 — Agitate**
Deepen the pain. Describe symptoms, consequences, and why this problem 
is more costly than it appears. Make the reader feel the friction.

**Step 3 — Solution**
Present your solution clearly — not generically. Explain the mechanism 
by which you solve the problem.

**Step 4 — CTA**
End with a single, specific call to action:
- Book a call or demo
- Start a free trial
- Subscribe to newsletter
- Download a resource

> **Pre-requisite:** Your LinkedIn profile must clearly articulate 
> your offer, its value, and who it is for *before* running conversion 
> content. The post drives traffic to the profile — the profile closes 
> the conversion.

---

---

### 6. Who This Framework Is (and Is Not) For

**Use this framework if:**
- You are building a qualified audience of ICP-matched buyers
- You want to be perceived as the go-to expert in a specific category
- You have a defined offer to sell (product, service, coaching, SaaS)

**Do not use this framework if:**
- Maximum follower count is your only goal
- You have no offer or monetisation mechanism
- You are unconcerned with being perceived as a category expert

---

## Why It Works for B2B SaaS

The Authority First framework is structurally suited to B2B SaaS 
for three interconnected reasons:

- **B2B buyers evaluate expertise before they evaluate product.** 
  In a market crowded with competing tools, the vendor who is 
  perceived as the category authority wins consideration before 
  the demo even happens. Leading with expert content builds this 
  perception at scale before any sales conversation begins.

- **Niche SaaS cannot afford a mismatched audience.** A productivity 
  tool for RevOps teams has no use for an audience of general 
  motivational content consumers. The 70% expert content weighting 
  acts as a natural ICP filter — only people who care about the 
  topic follow and engage, meaning every impression compounds toward 
  qualified pipeline rather than vanity metrics.

- **LinkedIn's 2026 algorithm favours depth over virality.** The 
  platform is actively deprioritising generic inspirational content 
  and surfacing expert, insight-driven posts to relevant professional 
  audiences — making the Authority First approach algorithmically 
  advantaged compared to traditional ToFu-heavy strategies.

---

## Tactical Takeaways

- **Audit your last 30 posts and categorise them by pillar.** If more 
  than 30% are broad personal or motivational content and fewer than 
  70% are genuine industry expertise posts, your content mix is working 
  against your commercial goals — rebalance immediately.

- **Source every expert post from direct experience, not research.** 
  Generic insights available from AI tools or industry blogs provide 
  no differentiation. The only content that builds real authority is 
  content that could only have been written by you — opinions, 
  observations, and frameworks derived from your own work.

- **Introduce personal stories only after your expertise positioning 
  is established.** Posting founder journey content before your 
  audience understands what you do and who you serve creates 
  connection without context — it does not convert. Sequence matters.

- **Use the PAS framework for every conversion post — no exceptions.** 
  Offer posts that open with a product pitch before establishing the 
  problem skip the emotional prerequisite for purchase intent. Problem 
  first, always.

- **Define one specific CTA per conversion post.** Asking readers to 
  book a call, start a trial, and subscribe to a newsletter in the 
  same post fractures attention and reduces conversion on all three. 
  One post, one goal.
