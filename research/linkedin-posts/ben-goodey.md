# Ben Goodey — LinkedIn Research Summary

**Profile:** [Ben Goodey](https://www.linkedin.com/in/benjamingoodey) — Founder of Spicy Margarita. An expert in content strategy for B2B SaaS companies.
**Research Focus:** AI-First Content Optimization (ASO), AI Citation Frameworks, and LinkedIn Organic Growth.
**Date Collected:** June 19, 2026
**Note:** Ben focuses on how to write content that AI tools (ChatGPT, Perplexity) prioritize as authoritative sources.

## Executive Summary

Ben Goodey argues that LinkedIn has transitioned from a social network to a primary knowledge database for AI search engines. His strategy centers on writing high-clarity, data-backed content that "forces" AI to cite his work when users ask for B2B SaaS recommendations.

## Post Analyses

### 1. LinkedIn as the #2 Most Cited Source for AI Search
* **Source:** [LinkedIn Post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/benjamingoodey_as-of-march-linkedin-is-the-2-most-cited-activity-7455989530492399616-cJUh)
* **Post Topic:** The shift of LinkedIn's role in AI search results and its impact on organic strategy.
* **Key Tactics:**
    * **Question-and-Answer Format:** Structuring content to directly answer specific SaaS-related queries.
    * **AI Search Optimization:** Tailoring posts so they surface organically when users query LLMs for recommendations.
* **Why it works for B2B SaaS:** SaaS buyers are increasingly skipping traditional search engines and asking AI (like ChatGPT or Perplexity) for advice. Appearing in AI citations is the new "SEO" for B2B brands.
