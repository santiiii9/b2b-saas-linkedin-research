# Mohamed Elidrysy — LinkedIn Research Summary

**Profile:** [Mohamed Elidrysy](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mohamedelidrysy) — CEO of PromiseClick. Focuses on bridging the gap between SaaS products and their users through transparent, high-value communication.
**Research Focus:** B2B SaaS organic growth, founder-led content, and building authority through product-first storytelling.
**Date Collected:** June 19, 2026
**Note:** Elidrysy advocates for "Building in Public" (serious B2B version) by sharing operational realities of SaaS rather than generic marketing advice.

## Executive Summary

Mohamed Elidrysy’s LinkedIn strategy is built on one core thesis: **B2B SaaS organic authority comes from proximity to the customer, not from abstract "thought leadership."** Key frameworks:

1. **Real-Time Customer Feedback Loop** — Turning genuine customer conversations into educational LinkedIn content.
2. **Product-Led Authority** — Focusing on product updates, building processes, and real insights rather than SEO tips or algorithm hacks.
3. **The Anti-Generic Rule** — Avoiding "guru" content and focusing on "hands-on" work that proves CEO competence.
